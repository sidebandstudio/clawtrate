Bot avatars

The three creature families of visual-language.md, section 5, drawn from the shared spec (packages/core/src/avatar.ts; js/creatures.js is its copy). Each family in each state at each size the app draws avatars at: 18 px in a card, 22 px in the title pill, 32 and 40 px in a sidebar row, 56 px on a pinned tile. Dark first, as the app opens, then light. The bar's Motion switch shows each state's still pose.
